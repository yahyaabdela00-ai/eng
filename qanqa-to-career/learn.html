Module 01 · Lesson 1.2

Vowels & Consonants

Learn the 5 vowel sounds and how they shape English words. Practice with common everyday examples.

Lesson 1.2 ● 14:32 ● HD

What you'll learn in this lesson

In English, the alphabet has 26 letters. Five of them are vowels (A, E, I, O, U) and the rest are consonants. Understanding the difference is the foundation of pronunciation.

"Every English word needs at least one vowel." Today's key idea

Practice phrases

Apple · Egg · Ice · Orange · Umbrella — five everyday words, one for each vowel. Repeat them after the instructor in the video.

Why this matters

Once you understand vowel and consonant sounds, you can read almost any new word in English — even ones you've never seen before. This is the gateway lesson to the rest of the course.
